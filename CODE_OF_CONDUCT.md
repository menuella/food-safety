# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be kind. We are building reference data that restaurants and their guests rely
on. Disagree on substance, not on people.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements here are about evidence, not motives.
- **Cite sources.** For anything touching allergens or legal declarations, a
  citation settles an argument faster than an opinion does.
- Accept that "no" is a legitimate answer, and give reasons when you say it.
- Respect that contributors work in different languages, legal systems and
  timezones — and that many are not writing in their first language.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sustained disruption, or
publishing someone's private information.

Also unacceptable, and specific to this project: **knowingly submitting
incorrect safety data**, or pressing for a change that would weaken an allergen
declaration without evidence. People with allergies read what we publish.

## Reporting

Email **hello@menuella.com**. Reports are handled privately by the maintainers.

We will respond as promptly as we can, keep the reporter's identity
confidential, and take action proportionate to the situation — from a private
word, through removing a comment, to a ban.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, and any space where
someone is representing the project.

---

*Short and specific by intent. If you would prefer a broader, formally worded
policy, the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org) is the
usual choice and we are happy to adopt it.*
